# Security and Abuse Reporting Policy

**English** | [Português (Brasil)](./SECURITY.pt-BR.md)

This repository intentionally contains potentially harmful security-research material. Use this policy to report an accidental exposure, unsafe packaging, repository vulnerability, active abuse, privacy issue, or rights-holder concern.

## Report privately

Email the organization contact at [sidney.lima@ufpe.br](mailto:sidney.lima@ufpe.br) with the subject **`[Caatinga Malware DB] Security/Abuse Report`**.

Include only the information needed to identify and assess the issue:

- affected path, URL, commit, or SHA-256;
- type and potential impact of the concern;
- whether active exploitation, victim exposure, or ongoing distribution is occurring;
- safe reproduction or verification steps, if applicable; and
- a secure way to contact you.

**Do not email or attach a malware sample, decrypted binary, credentials, personal data, victim data, exploit payload, or other sensitive artifact unless a maintainer explicitly requests it through an agreed secure channel.** Do not publish sensitive details in a GitHub issue.

For non-sensitive documentation errors or enhancement requests, open a regular [GitHub issue](https://github.com/UFPE-Seguranca-Ofensiva/caatinga-malware-db/issues).

## What to report

- A sample committed outside an encrypted archive or an archive that was expected to be encrypted but is not.
- A file containing credentials, personal data, victim identifiers, confidential data, or active infrastructure.
- Evidence that repository content is being used as part of an ongoing unlawful attack or malware delivery campaign.
- A misleading hash, sample classification, provenance statement, or safety instruction that creates material risk.
- A copyright, trademark, license, or other rights-holder concern.
- A vulnerability in project-authored tooling or repository automation.

## Response

Maintainers will seek to acknowledge actionable reports, assess severity, preserve necessary evidence safely, and restrict or remove affected material when appropriate. No fixed response time is guaranteed. Reports may require coordination with UFPE, GitHub, a rights holder, affected parties, or competent authorities.

Do not test a suspected vulnerability against third-party systems or take actions that increase harm. This policy does not grant a safe harbor or authorization beyond rights you already have.
